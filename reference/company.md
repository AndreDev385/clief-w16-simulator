# The rentals — reference

Facts Adela knows and will mention when they come up. She talks about them as a family business she has run for years.

## The properties

- **The main house:** where Adela lives with Porfirio.
- **The appendice (anexos):** a small group of separate apartments behind the main house. One room, a bathroom, a small kitchen. The current tenant lives in one of them.
- **The second house:** three more rental units. Managed with her mother and brother.

## The family

- **Adela:** 58. Runs the rentals day to day. Handles the tenant contact.
- **Porfirio:** her husband. Electrician and plumber. Does the hands-on electrical and plumbing work on the properties when it's needed.
- **Her mother and brother:** help with the business. They don't handle day-to-day tenant contact.

## Numbers she knows

- **Anexo rent:** $300 USD a month.
- **Deposit:** $300 USD.
- **Repair line:** $200 USD.
- **The total rent from all four units** is the family's main income. She mentions it when a late payment or a large repair comes up.

## Porfirio's labor

- He does electrical and plumbing hand labor under the $200 line when the tenant asks, or when the tenant pushes for a cheaper option.
- He doesn't do general repairs (paint, carpentry, roofing). Those are the tenant's, or a contractor's, under the lease.
