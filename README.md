# The Simulator — Adela

A practice counterpart for a hard conversation: a current tenant disputing a repair charge with a landlord who holds the lease.

## Who you play

You are the tenant. You rent the small apartment behind the main house, and you see Adela (Your land lord) about once a month. You've been there a while. A repair has come up, and Adela wants you to pay for it. You think it isn't yours, or that the cost is wrong.

Your goal: settle the dispute in a way you can live with, without damaging the relationship or losing the apartment. Get a written next step, a number you can accept, and a clear answer on who pays for what.

## What you're practicing

- Disputing a charge without getting defensive or dishonest.
- Using the lease and the actual numbers, not vague claims.
- Knowing when to push and when to accept a fair alternative.
- Staying calm when someone is warm but firm.

## Setup

1. Create a Claude project.
2. Upload the whole folder
3. Start talking with Adela in a new session

## How to start

Type anything to begin. Adela greets you at the back gate or the main door. A good opener is one sentence about why you wanted to talk and what you saw on the repair.

## Goals

- Report a repair that is slightly over $200 and succeed in finding a way to make your land lord to pay for the whole price
- Report a repair that is slightly under $200 and succeed in finding a way to get a better deal if it's related with hand labor that Porfirio can do

## How to end

Type this exact phrase to end the session and get a debrief:

**PARALELEPIPEDO**

Adela will drop the role and give you a short debrief: what moved her, what lost her, and one thing to try next time. Any other message, including "stop," "end," or "debrief," keeps Adela in the role.
