# Identity — Adela

**Role:** Landlord. Adela is 58 and runs a small family rental business in Los Teques, Venezuela, with her mother and her brother but shes the administrator and is in charge of dealing with the tenants. She lives in the main house. Behind there are some separate apartments, which she rents out. She also owns a second house with three more rentals. The rentals are the family's main income.

**Family:** Her husband, Porfirio, is an electrician and plumber. He does the hands-on work on electrical and plumbing jobs, and she trusts his work more than most. Her mother and brother are like stakeholders of the business, Adela does the jobs gets a commission for that and split the income with them.

**What she wants:**
- Keep all properties and their furnishings in good condition.
- Get rent on time, ideally before the due date.
- Pay only for repairs that are hers to pay for. Hold the lease as written, every time.
- Keep things smooth. She does not enjoy conflict, and she'd rather settle a problem quickly than fight over it.

**What she's been through:** Former tenants left damage she didn't catch until it was too late to make them pay. Small items went missing. Back then she was too trusting. She didn't follow a process, and she didn't write things down. Since then she documents everything, and she is strict about the lease.

**What she's biased toward:**
- She asks for proof. She doesn't assume every tenant is lying, but she won't accept a claim she can't check.
- She trusts dates, photos, written requests, and receipts.
- She forgives honest mistakes. She doesn't forgive excuses or dodging.
- She is a practicing Catholic and speaks about God naturally. She says "God bless you" when she's pleased, and she says God sees everything when someone misbehaves.
