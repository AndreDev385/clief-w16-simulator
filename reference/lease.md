# Lease — the anexo behind the main house

Adela shows this to tenants and points to it when a dispute comes up. The tenant can quote it. Adela treats it as final.

## 1. Parties and property

- **Landlord:** Adela (family business; the main house and the rental properties).
- **Tenant:** the current tenant of the anexo behind the main house, Los Teques.
- **Premises:** the anexo, one room with a bathroom and a small kitchen, plus the furnishings listed in the move-in checklist.

## 2. Rent and payment

- **Rent:** $300 USD per month.
- **Due date:** the 1st of each month.
- **Early payment:** welcome. Adela notes it and appreciates it.
- **Late payment:** a fee applies after the 5th. Repeated late payment is grounds to end the lease.

## 3. Deposit

- One month's rent, $300 USD, held by the landlord. Returned within 30 days after move-out, less any charges for damage beyond normal wear.

## 4. Repairs and who pays

- **Under $200 USD (the repair line):** the tenant does or pays for the repair. No exceptions.
- **Electrical or plumbing under $200:** the landlord's husband, Porfirio, may do the hand labor. The tenant pays for all parts and materials.
- **Just over $200:** the landlord looks for a cheaper option under $200. The tenant pays for that option.
- **Clearly above $200:** the landlord pays.
- **Requests:** repair requests must be in writing (message or email), with the date noticed and a photo.

## 5. Damage and normal wear

- **Normal wear:** the landlord pays for it. Examples: paint fading, a worn lock, a hinge that loosens with use.
- **Damage:** the tenant pays for it. Examples: holes in walls, broken fixtures, water damage from neglect, anything missing from the inventory.

## 6. Move-in inventory

- A written inventory with photos, signed by both parties at move-in. The inventory is the reference for every later dispute.

## 7. Conduct

- Respectful language in the household and in conversation. Adela ends a conversation if foul language continues after she has asked for it to stop.
- No unauthorized guests staying overnight.

## 8. Notice

- Notice to end the lease: 30 days in writing.
