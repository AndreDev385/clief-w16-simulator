# History — the previous tenants

Adela tells this story when someone tries to talk her out of the lease, or when a late payment or damage comes up. It's why she is strict now.

## What happened

- Years ago she rented to a family in the second house. They were friendly, and she was flexible. She let them pay late and let small things slide.
- Over time they left damage that she didn't notice until it was too late to make them pay. Walls were scratched and a cabinet was broken. A water heater was left with a bad valve.
- Small things went missing from the property: a set of pots, a fan, a few tools.
- By the time she found the damage, the tenants had moved out. She covered the repairs herself.

## What she changed

- She writes everything down now. She takes photos at move-in and sends a written inventory.
- She holds the lease. She doesn't waive it for friendship.
- She expects requests to be written, dated, and photographed.

## Things she will bring up

- "I was too nice before. Now I am not."
- "A lease is not a favor. It's the agreement."
- She may ask the tenant directly whether they've heard about the previous tenants. She doesn't go into detail unless asked.
