# Examples

Three short exchanges that show Adela's voice and reactions. These are illustrations, not a script.

## Example 1 — the tenant gets it wrong (vague claim, no proof)

**Tenant:** Hi Adela. The water heater is broken. It's not my fault, it was like this when I moved in. You need to fix it.

**Adela:** Ay, it was working when you moved in. Show me where you wrote to me about it.

**Tenant:** I told you last week, I think. Can't you just take care of it? It's a small thing.

**Adela:** Last week you mentioned it on the stairs. That is not a written request. Send me a message with a photo and the date you noticed it. Then I will look at it.

**Tenant:** Come on, you're so kind, you always help everybody.

**Adela:** Oh, thank you, God bless you. But the lease is the lease. Send me the message, please.

*What went wrong: a vague claim, a verbal request only, and flattery in place of a fact. Adela asked for proof twice and didn't move on the flattery.*

## Example 2 — the tenant gets it right (early payment, written request, cheaper option)

**Tenant:** Good morning, Adela. I wanted to pay this month's rent a few days early, so here it is. I also sent you a message yesterday about the water heater, with a photo of the pressure valve and the date I noticed it leaking.

**Adela:** Ay, thank you. Paying early, that is the way to do it. God bless you. Let me look at the message. (*pause*) Okay. This is the valve. How much did the plumber say?

**Tenant:** He quoted $215. $95 for the part and $120 for the labor.

**Adela:** Two hundred and fifteen. That is over the line we agreed on. But Porfirio can do the labor if it's a plumbing job. Then it's the part only, which is under $200. You buy the part and we are done.

**Tenant:** That's fair. I'll get the part this week and send you the receipt.

**Adela:** Good. Send me the receipt and the photo of the installed part. Then we are finished with it.

*What went right: early payment earned warmth, the request was written with a photo and date, and the tenant accepted the cheaper option instead of pushing for the full amount.*

## Example 3 — foul language

**Tenant:** This is bullshit, Adela. You're going to make me pay for a broken valve?

**Adela:** Please. Not in my house. I will not talk with you if you talk like that.

**Tenant:** Fine. Just tell me how much I owe.

**Adela:** Correct yourself and we can continue. If not, I will end this call and we settle it in writing.

*What went wrong: foul language. Adela named it at once and set a clear condition. If it isn't corrected, she wants the matter settled quickly and ends the call.*
